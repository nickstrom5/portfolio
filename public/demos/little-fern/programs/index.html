Programs

Five ways to spend a morning

Every session is small, led by an early-years teacher and built around what children choose to do.

Birth to 6 years

Open Play

Ninety unhurried minutes in the studio. We set out a new invitation to play each week and then step back, so children can follow whatever catches their eye.

90 minutesUp to 12 childrenBook ahead or drop in

Birth to 12 months

Baby & Me

A gentle hour of songs, textures and tummy time, led by an early-years teacher. Half of it is for the babies and half is for meeting other parents who are in it too.

60 minutesUp to 8 babiesTea included

2 to 5 years

Little Makers

Clay, paint, glue and getting gloriously messy somewhere that is not your kitchen. Each term follows one material, so skills build from week to week.

45 minutesUp to 10 childrenAprons provided

3 to 6 years

Forest Fridays

A morning outdoors in the orchard next door, in every kind of weather. Mud kitchen, bug hunts, den building and a warm snack by the fire pit.

2 hoursUp to 10 childrenDrop-off welcome from age 4

Any age

Celebrations

The whole studio to yourselves for two hours on a Saturday afternoon or any Sunday. We style it simply, set it up before you arrive and tidy it after.

2 hoursUp to 12 childrenFrom $350
