Before you ask

Can I try it before joining?

Yes. Your first Open Play session is free. Book a first visit from the Contact page and we will find a quiet time to show you round.

What if we miss a session?

Cancel up to two hours before and the session goes back on your account. Unused membership sessions roll over for one month.

Can I pause my membership?

Any time, for as long as you need. There is no joining fee and no contract, so you can also stop altogether with a week's notice.

Do grandparents or nannies need their own pass?

No. The price covers one child and whichever grown-up brings them that day. A second adult is always welcome at no charge.
