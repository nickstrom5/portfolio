Questions parents ask

Do I need to book ahead?

No. Walk in any time we're open. On rainy weekends we can fill up, so it's worth a quick call before you set off.

Can I bring my own food?

Baby food and birthday cake for a booked party, yes. Otherwise please use the café. Our kitchen is nut-free, so we ask that outside snacks stay in the bag.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes. Your wristband is good until we close, so nap time and lunch runs are no problem.

Can I pause a membership?

Any time, for up to three months a year. Tell us at the desk or send a message from the Visit page.
