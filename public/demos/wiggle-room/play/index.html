Play zones

Four ways to wear them out

Every zone is included with entry. Move between them as often as you like.

Ages 0 to 3

Tiny Town

A gated soft-play world for crawlers and new walkers, away from the big-kid traffic.

  • Padded floor, low climbers and a baby ball pit
  • Sensory wall with lights, textures and sounds
  • Nursing nook and changing room right beside it
Ages 4 to 10

The Big Climb

Three levels of nets, bridges and tunnels that end in a pair of racing slides.

  • Twin 20-foot racing slides
  • Spider net, rope bridge and lookout tower
  • Grown-ups fit too, and yes, you are allowed
All ages

Makers Corner

A calmer table for building, drawing and the craft of the day, included with entry.

  • A new craft every day at 11am and 3pm
  • Giant foam blocks and magnetic tiles
  • Take your masterpiece home
For the grown-ups

The Perch Café

Proper espresso and a seat with a clear view of the whole play floor.

  • Local roast coffee, tea and fresh pastries
  • Kids' lunch boxes, fruit pots and toasties
  • Nut-free kitchen, with gluten-free options every day

Good to know

House rules, the short version

Socks on

Kids and grown-ups. Grip socks are $3 at the desk.

Stay close

A grown-up stays with their children for the whole visit.

Step-free

Ramp entry, a lift to every floor and an accessible restroom.

Feeling ill?

Stay home and we'll move your booking for free.

Ready to play?

Walk in any day. Sign the waiver ahead of time and skip the line.
