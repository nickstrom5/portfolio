## An AI experiment

Damp is part of the same experiment as Clam: how far can AI carry a real product? Roughly 95 percent of the strategy, copy, Swift code, tests, site and launch material was generated by Claude Code from one instruction, "follow the same process we did for Clam, but make an entirely new app idea". I directed, reviewed and made the calls. The full process is on the [AI/Projects page](/apps/#damp).

## The idea

Every app in this category sells quitting. Most people just want fewer drinking nights and less regret the morning after. Damp counts nights, not drinks. At nine o'clock your phone asks "Dry tonight?" and you answer from the notification. A dry night adds to the streak and to the money you kept, worked out from your own numbers. A drinking night is logged as a number, not a relapse: the streak resets, the savings and the history stay.

## What got built

- The SwiftUI app: a nine-step onboarding that turns drinks a week and price per drink into a yearly figure before the paywall, a StoreKit 2 subscription flow with a lifetime option, the home screen with a week strip and one button, a drinks log sheet, milestone share cards and settings with a Help section.
- A nightly local notification with two actions, so logging never needs the app open, plus a Home Screen widget and a Siri and Action Button intent.
- Everything derived from a local log: streaks, money kept and calories skipped are recomputed, never stored, and nothing leaves the phone.
- Unit tests for the streak and savings maths, a CI build, and a screenshot mode that opens any screen with seeded data.
- The launch kit: strategy and naming notes, App Store listing, launch videos, creator outreach, and a landing site with a drinking cost calculator, two guides, support, privacy and terms on GitHub Pages.

## Status

In development. The app builds and passes its tests in the simulator and the site is live at usedamp.app. Next is the first run on a physical iPhone and the App Store Connect setup. Free for seven days, then a yearly, monthly or lifetime purchase.
