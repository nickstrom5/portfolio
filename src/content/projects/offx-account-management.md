## The engagement

From July 2024 to February 2025 I was the account manager for OFFX, an office experience company serving business clients.

## What I did

- Served as the main point of contact for client accounts, keeping relationships warm and expectations clear.
- Tracked every order from placement through delivery, chasing the steps in between so nothing stalled.
- Handled invoicing and the follow-up that keeps accounts current.

## Why it matters

Account management is operations with a customer's name on it. The job is making sure the client never has to ask where their order is.
