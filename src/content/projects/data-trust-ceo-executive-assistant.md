## The engagement

The CEO of a nonprofit that stewards biological data infrastructure hired me through Upwork in January 2026 as a U.S.-based executive assistant. The first contract ran through April; the client brought me back in July 2026 and the engagement is ongoing.

## What I do

- Own the calendar across time zones, including the scheduling back-and-forth with the people the CEO needs to meet.
- Triage the inbox so the CEO sees what needs a decision and nothing else.
- Prepare meeting materials, track action items and chase follow-ups until they close.

## Why it matters

Being asked back is the review that counts. The role works when the CEO stops thinking about logistics entirely.
