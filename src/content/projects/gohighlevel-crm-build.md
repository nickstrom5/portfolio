## The problem

Leads came in from ads, a webinar and a website form, and all of them landed in the founder's personal inbox. Follow-up depended on who remembered, and nobody could say how many leads were sitting untouched.

## What I did

- Designed a single pipeline in GoHighLevel with stages that matched how the team actually sold, not how the template shipped.
- Built the funnels, booking calendar and payment links, and wired every lead source into the pipeline with Zapier.
- Wrote the follow-up sequences: SMS and email nudges over the first two weeks, with hand-off rules for when a human should step in.
- Set up a dashboard the founder checks each morning instead of opening the inbox.

## Outcome

Every lead now has an owner and a next step. The founder stopped being the bottleneck, and the team could hire a second closer without rebuilding anything.
