## The engagement

Blackdove is a platform for displaying digital art on screens. From January 2022 to December 2024 I worked with the team as a junior marketing manager.

## What I did

- Guided prospective clients from their first contact through to becoming customers, keeping the follow-up consistent and personal.
- Supported the marketing work that brought those clients in.
- Kept the customer journey organized in the CRM so the team could see where every lead stood.
