## The idea

Most local businesses either have no website or a broken one, and the click from Google Maps goes to the next shop in the list. LaunchNeat sells the fix at a price a salon or a food truck will say yes to: $99 for the first year including the domain, $50 a year after that, with a tight, clearly stated scope.

## What I did

- Set the offer, the two price points, the in-scope and out-of-scope lists and the fifteen niches.
- Directed Claude Code to build the marketing site and fifteen fictional demo businesses, each with its own copy, palette and font pairing rendered through one template.
- Rebuilt the first version, which started on an app scaffold, as a plain static site of about 540 KB with no backend.
- Registered launchneat.com and set up the static deploy and SEO pass.
