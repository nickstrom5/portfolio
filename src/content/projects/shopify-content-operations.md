## The problem

New products launched late because listings, images and landing pages were done by whoever had time. The blog had gone quiet, and nobody was checking whether any of it drove sales.

## What I did

- Set up a launch checklist and a content calendar so every product had listing copy, retouched images and a landing page ready before launch day.
- Kept the Shopify storefront and WordPress blog updated weekly, including seasonal collections and promotions.
- Built the Klaviyo flows for launches and set up analytics goals so the team could see which pages converted.

## Outcome

Launches went out on schedule, and the team had a monthly report tying content work to revenue for the first time.
