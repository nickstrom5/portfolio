## The problem

A research program with dozens of contributors across several universities had no single view of what was due, to whom, and when. Reporting to the funder was assembled by hand every quarter from email threads and spreadsheets.

## What I did

- Set up the program in Asana with one portfolio per work package, a shared milestone calendar and a reporting template that mirrored the funder's format.
- Ran weekly status cycles: chased updates, flagged risks early and kept the principal investigators out of the administrative weeds.
- Coordinated vendors, contractors and subcontract invoices so nothing stalled on paperwork.
- Documented every process in Confluence so new staff could onboard without a hand-holding period.

## Outcome

This became one of my longest engagements, running past 3,000 hours. Quarterly reports went from a week of scrambling to a two-day routine, and the program passed its funder reviews without findings.
