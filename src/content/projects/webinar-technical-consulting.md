## The engagement

An independent presenter hired me through Upwork in July 2026 as a webinar and technical consultant. The contract is ongoing.

## What I do

- Advise on and configure the webinar setup so it runs the same way every time.
- Handle the technical side of each session so the presenter only has to present.
- Be the person to call when something on screen does not work.

## Why it matters

Presenters lose audiences to technical problems, not content. Having someone own the technical side lets the content land.
