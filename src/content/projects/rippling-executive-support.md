## The engagement

From March 2019 to March 2020 I supported several executives at Rippling as an executive assistant.

## What I did

- Owned calendars and scheduling across time zones for multiple leaders.
- Took on project coordination tasks so executives could hand off follow-through and stay focused on decisions.
- Handled the administrative details that keep a fast-growing company's leadership team running.

## Why it matters

Executive support is where I learned to anticipate rather than react. That habit carries into every operations role I take on.
