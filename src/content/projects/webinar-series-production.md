## The problem

The department wanted a polished monthly webinar but had no one who could run the technical side, manage speakers and produce a replay without burning a week of staff time each month.

## What I did

- Owned the calendar: speaker outreach, prep calls, run-of-show documents and rehearsal slots.
- Ran each live session through OBS with branded lower-thirds, slides and a moderated Q&A, and handled the inevitable audio surprises in real time.
- Edited each replay in Premiere Pro with title cards from After Effects, and delivered a short promo cut for social.
- Managed registration and reminder emails so attendance held up month over month.

## Outcome

Sessions ran without a technical hitch for the full season and consistently drew over 200 live attendees. The department renewed for the following year.
