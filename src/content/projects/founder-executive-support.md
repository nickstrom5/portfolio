## The problem

The founder was spending mornings on email and scheduling and evenings on expense receipts, with hiring squeezed in between. Good candidates were slipping through because nobody followed up.

## What I did

- Took over the inbox and calendar with clear rules for what reached the founder and what I handled directly.
- Set up expense capture into QuickBooks so month-end reconciliation became a review rather than a hunt.
- Built a hiring pipeline in Notion, wrote the job posts, screened applicants and scheduled interviews, then ran onboarding for each new hire.
- Handled first-line customer service so client questions got an answer the same day.

## Outcome

The founder got their mornings back, and the agency nearly tripled headcount over the engagement without a recruiter.
