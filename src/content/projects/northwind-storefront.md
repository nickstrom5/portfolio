## The problem

Northwind's Shopify theme had accumulated years of apps and custom scripts. Pages took several seconds to load on mobile, and every change risked breaking checkout.

## What I built

- A headless Next.js storefront on the Shopify Storefront API with static product pages and incremental revalidation.
- A small component library so the marketing team could assemble landing pages without a developer.
- Analytics and A/B test hooks, plus a migration plan that kept the old theme live until cutover.

## Outcome

Core Web Vitals moved into the green on mobile and conversion improved in the first quarter after launch. The team now ships landing pages themselves.
