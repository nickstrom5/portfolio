## The engagement

BARBRI produces a large library of video courses taught by law professors. Between March 2023 and April 2024 I was the project manager for video production, responsible for the post-production pipeline behind hundreds of those courses.

## What I did

- Tracked every course through post-production, from raw recordings to published lessons, so the team always knew what was in edit, in review and ready to ship.
- Managed the production timelines and flagged slippage early enough to fix it.
- Worked directly with professors and the creative team, coordinating recording schedules, revisions and approvals.

## Why it matters

High-volume content production fails quietly when nobody owns the tracking. This role was about making the pipeline visible and keeping a large, opinionated group of contributors moving to the same schedule.
