## The problem

Students enrolled through a PDF form and paid by bank transfer. Staff reconciled payments manually and had no view of who had actually started their courses.

## What I built

- A React portal with enrollment, Stripe checkout and installment plans.
- Progress data pulled from the school's Moodle instance and shown to students and advisors.
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility pass, including keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels.

## Outcome

Enrollment moved fully online and payment reconciliation dropped to a daily automated report.
