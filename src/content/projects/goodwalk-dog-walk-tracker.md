## The idea

Your dog needs a walk every day. Good Walk turns that into a streak: a daily target, a reminder at the time you pick, and a one-tap "Walked" straight from the lock screen. Your dog's photo is on everything. There is no collar to buy, no map to draw and no account to create.

## What I did

- Set the concept and the constraints, then directed Claude Code through the screens, the reminder and streak logic, and the launch plan.
- Had the landing site built first: how it works, pricing, an FAQ, three guides on how long and how often to walk a dog, plus privacy and terms.
- Registered getgoodwalk.app and set the launch target for Walk Your Dog Week, 1 to 7 October 2026.

## Why it matters

It is the third app in the same experiment as Clam and Lume, and the fastest so far: from one sentence to a reviewable site in an evening. The full story is on the [AI/Projects page](/apps/#goodwalk).
