## The problem

Patients were booking by phone and joining visits through emailed links that regularly expired. No-shows were high and staff spent hours a day on scheduling.

## What I built

- A Flutter app with provider availability, booking, reminders and in-app video visits.
- Secure messaging with read receipts and attachment support.
- Firebase Auth with phone verification, Firestore security rules reviewed against the clinic's compliance checklist, and audit logging in Cloud Functions.
- Store submissions for both platforms, including the health-data disclosures each store requires.

## Outcome

No-shows fell after reminders and one-tap joining went live. The clinic now onboards new patients through the app instead of the phone.
