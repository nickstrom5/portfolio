## The engagement

In May 2026 a sales organization running roughly 100 reps hired me through Upwork as a full-time senior developer for their automation stack: Zapier, HubSpot and Keap. The contract ran full-time until September 2026 and closed with client feedback.

## What I did

- Built and maintained the Zapier workflows that move leads, bookings and rep activity between HubSpot, Keap and the tools the team lives in.
- Kept the CRM pipelines clean enough that leadership could trust the numbers without manual clean-up.
- Replaced recurring manual steps with automations, and documented each one so the team can maintain it.

## Why it matters

At 100 reps, every manual step is multiplied by a hundred. The job was removing those steps so the sales floor sells and the data stays right.
